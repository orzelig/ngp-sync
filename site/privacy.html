Privacy Policy

Effective: November 6, 2026

NGP ("we") operates the NGP browser and the ngp.one services. The short version: no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data. We collect the minimum needed to run sign-in, sync, and updates.

What we collect

  • X sign-in (only if you enable sync). When you log in with X we receive your public profile basics: X user id, handle, display name, and avatar. We do not receive your X password and we do not request your email.
  • Sync data (only if you enable sync). The browser data you choose to sync — such as bookmarks, history, passwords, open tabs, and settings — is stored on our servers so your devices can share it. It is encrypted in transit (TLS). For end-to-end encryption, set a sync passphrase in the browser; then we store data we cannot read.
  • Tokens and device links. We issue sign-in tokens to your browser (they expire; currently 30 days) and keep minimal records needed to operate them.
  • Server logs. Like any server, ours briefly logs technical details of requests (IP address, user agent, timestamps) for operations, debugging, and abuse prevention. Logs are routinely discarded.
  • Update checks. The browser periodically fetches a small static file from ngp.one to learn about new versions; the request carries no account identity. Downloads are served from GitHub, which sees the request like any website you visit.

What we don't collect

  • No browsing telemetry or analytics in the browser. Your browsing stays on your device unless you turn on sync — and then it goes only to our sync server, for you.
  • The ngp.one website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Third parties

Sign-in uses X; installing extensions talks to Google's Chrome Web Store; downloads come from GitHub; our servers run on Google Cloud (us-central1) with TLS certificates from Let's Encrypt. Each handles data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we share it with no one except these infrastructure providers as needed to run the Service.

Retention and deletion

Synced data and account records are kept while you use the Service. Turning off NGP sync in the browser removes its local credentials; to have your server-side data deleted, contact us (below) with your X handle and we will remove it. Tokens expire on their own. This is an early-stage service: we may also reset or delete stored sync data as part of maintenance — keep your own copies of anything important.

Security

Everything is served over TLS; access to servers is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure, and this is experimental software — do not store anything in sync whose loss or exposure you cannot afford, or use a sync passphrase.

Your choices

  • Sync is off by default — "Don't use sync" keeps the browser fully local.
  • "Turn off NGP sync" in the profile menu disconnects the account.
  • A sync passphrase makes synced data unreadable to us.
  • You can request deletion of your server-side data at any time.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy by posting a revised version here with a new effective date.

Contact

Privacy requests: open an issue at github.com/orzelig/ngp-releases (or request a private channel there and we will provide one).
